Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a lost item?

Click Report Lost Item in the navbar, fill in the details like description, category, location, and upload a photo if available. Our AI will start matching immediately.

How does AI matching work?

Our system analyzes item descriptions, categories, colors, brands, and locations across all reports. When a potential match is found, both parties are notified instantly.

How do I claim a found item?

Navigate to the Found Items page, find your item, and submit a claim with ownership proof - like identifying marks, brand details, or serial numbers.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use JWT authentication and all data is encrypted. Only verified SIBAU students can access the platform, and claims are reviewed by admin before approval.

Where do I pick up my item?

Once your claim is approved, visit the Lost & Found Office at the Knowledge center, 1st Floor, SIBAU Main Campus during working hours (Mon-Fri 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM).

Can I message the finder?

Yes! Our built-in messaging system lets you communicate directly with the person who found your item to coordinate details and verify ownership.

Don't see your item?

New items are reported every day. Submit a detailed lost report and our AI will keep searching for you - you'll be notified the moment a match is found.
